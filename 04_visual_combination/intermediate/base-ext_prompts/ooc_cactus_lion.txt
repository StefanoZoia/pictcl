A single cactus-lion stands on a flat desert ground. Its body has the shape of a standing lion with four legs and a tail, but the whole body is made of green cactus flesh with vertical ribs and many sharp spines. The head is a lion head with a short snout and open mouth, and around the head is a mane made of thick cactus pads covered in needles. The paws look like lion paws, but each toe ends in a cactus spine instead of a claw.
