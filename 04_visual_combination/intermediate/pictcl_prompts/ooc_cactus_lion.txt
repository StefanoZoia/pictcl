A single cactus-lion hybrid stands on sandy desert ground: it has the body shape and four legs of a lion, with a feline head facing forward and an open mouth showing teeth as if roaring, but its skin is green like a cactus and covered with short spines. A thick cactus trunk forms its torso, and the mane around its neck is made of clustered cactus pads with prickly needles instead of fur. The tail ends in a small round cactus bud with spines.
