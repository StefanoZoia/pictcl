A single sushi roll shaped like a sheep stands on a plain white background: the body is a thick cylinder of rice wrapped in a dark seaweed strip, the cut ends show rice with a pink fish center, and four short legs are made from small rice blocks. The head is a rounded rice ball with two small pointed nori ears, two black sesame seed eyes, and a small nori nose, and the top of the roll is covered with many small white rice grains to look like wool.
