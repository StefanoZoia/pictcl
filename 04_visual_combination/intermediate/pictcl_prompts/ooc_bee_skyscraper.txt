A single hybrid object stands upright like a tall city skyscraper, but its outer surface is the striped yellow-and-black body of a bee. The base is wide like an insect abdomen, and the tower narrows slightly toward the top. Two clear bee wings stick out from opposite sides near the upper third of the tower. The top ends in a bee head shape with two short antennae, and small dark windows are arranged in straight vertical rows along the striped body like building windows.
