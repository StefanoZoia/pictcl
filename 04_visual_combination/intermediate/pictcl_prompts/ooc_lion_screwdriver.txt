A single hybrid object sits on a plain surface: it has the head and front body of a lion with a mane, ears, eyes, nose, and open mouth showing teeth, while the back half turns into a metal screwdriver handle and a straight shaft. The screwdriver shaft extends from where the lion’s back would be and ends in a flat tip shaped like a slotted screwdriver. The lion’s front legs rest on the surface, and the screwdriver part points away from the lion’s head.
