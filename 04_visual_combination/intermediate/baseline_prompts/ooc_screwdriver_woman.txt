A single hybrid object is centered on a plain white background: it has the long metal shaft and flat-head tip of a screwdriver, but the handle is shaped like a standing woman. The woman-shaped handle has a head with simple facial features, a neck, a torso, two arms held close to the sides, and two legs forming the lower part of the grip.
