A single hybrid object stands upright like a tall glass-and-steel skyscraper, but the tower is shaped like a bee: it has a rounded head at the top with two short antennae, two large clear wings attached near the upper floors, and a tapered abdomen forming the lower part of the building. The building’s exterior has alternating black and yellow horizontal bands like bee stripes, and rows of windows follow the bands. Six thin legs extend from the lower sides of the tower and touch the ground like building supports.
